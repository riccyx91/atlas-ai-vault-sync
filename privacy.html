ATLAS AI Vault Sync

Privacy policy

This policy covers ATLAS AI Vault Sync, a desktop workflow operated by a single person using rclone to sync selected files between their computer and their Google Drive.

Google Drive data and permissions

rclone uses Google OAuth to obtain permission to call the Google Drive API. Depending on the permissions granted and the operation configured, rclone can read file contents and metadata such as names, sizes and modification times. It can also create, update or delete files and folders as needed for the configured sync. The permission granted by Google can cover more files than the folders selected for a sync. Drive data is used to compare files and carry out the operator’s file operations.

Storage, sharing and retention

Synced copies are stored on the operator’s computer and in their Google Drive. The desktop workflow keeps OAuth tokens in the local rclone configuration to maintain access. File transfers for this workflow go directly between rclone and Google Drive. ATLAS does not sell Google user data or share it for advertising. The GitHub Pages site does not receive Drive file data or OAuth credentials.

Synced files and any copies remain until removed by the operator, subject to the retention and trash settings of the systems where they are stored. To stop future access, the operator can disable the sync and remove the app’s access from their Google Account. Revoking access does not delete copies already synced. When retiring the configuration, the operator can also remove the local rclone credentials.

Public site and privacy questions

This information site uses no analytics scripts, tracking cookies or forms of its own. GitHub hosts the site and processes requests under its privacy statement. For privacy questions, use the support email shown on the Google OAuth consent screen.
